## Choose the right instructions

This site's tutorial describes a recorded development snapshot. For historical installers, follow the notes attached to that exact [evaluation release](../downloads/). Source-branch prerequisites do not retroactively qualify older packages.

- [Getting started](getting-started/): prerequisites, local accounts and recovery.
- [Create your first project](getting-started/first-project/): a concrete Launchpad workflow.
- [Full canonical user guide](https://github.com/draconianneocoder-cpu/GoPMgr/blob/1ca5ffaed63446b35b197a6083d03899c0143f55/docs/user-guide.md): tasks, reports, imports and recovery.
- [Build from source](https://github.com/draconianneocoder-cpu/GoPMgr/blob/1ca5ffaed63446b35b197a6083d03899c0143f55/docs/INSTALL.md#run--build-from-source): current toolchain and native prerequisites.

The full manuals stay in the repository. This companion site is a curated starting point, with status and source evidence on each page.
