This procedure is derived from the reviewed user guide. It has not been exercised against every native installer. Use a source build or evaluate your release's matching instructions.

## Preconditions

Use a supported development environment, a backed-up test account and fictional project data. Complete local account creation and save the recovery codes first. Do not use this walkthrough to migrate a production project.

## Create the workspace

1. Sign in to your local account. The Portfolio dashboard lists your projects.
2. Choose **New Project** to open the Launchpad.
3. Select an industry and sub-category appropriate to the test. Choose **Continue** to retain each selection and advance.
4. Select a methodology, then provide the project's details. Use a name such as **Bridge planning exercise** with fictional dates and costs.
5. Review the proposed starter artifacts. Deselect any you do not need.
6. On the final step, review the blueprint, business-calendar policy and IANA time zone before creating the project. Calendar policy controls working days and holidays; the time zone controls schedule boundaries.
7. Return to the Portfolio and open the project card to enter its Dashboard.

## Expected outcome

The new test project appears in the Portfolio and opens into a workspace for its charts and documents. Check the chosen calendar and time zone in Project Settings before entering a schedule.

## Prefer a blank project?

**Start with a blank project** on the first Launchpad step skips the wizard and asks for a name and description. The canonical guide says this creates an empty project with the US business calendar; review and change that policy in Project Settings when needed.

## If the result differs

Stop before adding important data. Compare your build/release to the source links below. Report a non-sensitive reproduction through [community](../../../community/); omit databases, recovery codes, private paths and certificates. Do not delete account metadata or overwrite an existing project to make the tutorial match.
