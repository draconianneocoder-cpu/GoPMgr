## Decision, as stated by the maintainer

> As the project maintainer and owner of GoPMgr, I formally ACCEPT ADR-012
> v0.2 — DataFusion Analytics Replacement, including its revised architectural
> direction, security requirements, feasibility specification, migration
> strategy, and acceptance criteria.

The acceptance covers:

- decisions DF-01 to DF-15, as revised in v0.2;
- amendments DF-A01 to DF-A14;
- the test matrix DF-001 to DF-065;
- the architecture-preservation and coding-agent instructions;
- continued compatibility with the accepted ADR-010 decisions;
- the continued authority of SQLCipher over encrypted project data.

> ADR-012 v0.2 is hereby ACCEPTED as the binding target architecture, subject
> to its documented feasibility gates and reserved owner decisions.

It supersedes the earlier proposed versions for future design work, which are
kept as historical records.

> It does not mean that DataFusion has passed feasibility testing, that a
> specific analytics implementation has been selected, or that DuckDB has
> already been replaced.

### Direction

The objective is to remove GoPMgr's production dependency on DuckDB while
preserving or improving:

- functionality and correctness;
- security;
- performance;
- maintainability, interoperability and distribution quality.

DataFusion and Arrow are the preferred candidates, but the evidence decides.
Three alternatives are evaluated:

1. DuckDB, as the reference;
2. Rust DataFusion for the existing workloads;
3. native Go aggregation for deterministic calculations, combined with a Rust
   DataFusion worker for columnar and advanced analytics.

> Do not force simple financial calculations into DataFusion when a native Go
> implementation can demonstrably provide superior correctness, performance,
> or simplicity.

Further constraints on the direction:

- The worker runs behind the language-neutral plugin architecture, over
  authenticated native gRPC and the approved local IPC.
- No DataFusion, Arrow or Iceberg replacement of SQLCipher.
- Iceberg is not a prerequisite.

### Authorization

**DF0 is AUTHORIZED**, kept isolated from production application behavior. It
covers:

- integrating the accepted documentation;
- the full DF0 investigation;
- isolated Go and Rust proof-of-concept modules;
- benchmarks of all three alternatives;
- Rust gRPC over the accepted Windows named-pipe transport;
- independent oracles and fixtures;
- all supported input formats;
- security, dependency, licensing, resource and packaging feasibility reviews;
- an evidence-backed recommendation.

**Not authorized** (each needs separate approval and its gates):

- production integration;
- mandatory dependency changes;
- SQLCipher migrations;
- DuckDB removal;
- release packaging changes.

### Mandatory quality requirements (summary of directive §4)

1. Preserve every existing analytical capability and user-visible result,
   unless an approved correction is backed by independent evidence.
2. Exact money, deterministic rounding, currency distinctions, and EVM
   availability semantics, including zero, missing, negative and overflow
   values.
3. Strict separation of authoritative data and nonauthoritative computation.
4. No unauthorized filesystem or network access, no plaintext temporary-data
   exposure, and no unbounded resource use.
5. Memory, CPU, concurrency, cancellation and process-lifecycle limits, each
   with a verifiable test.
6. Fully functional offline.
7. Supported-platform commitments unchanged unless separately approved.
8. Measure both compute and complete user-observed latency.
9. Every accepted file type keeps its import behavior and fidelity.
10. All 65 tests kept, with more added for new risks. An unexecuted test is
    NOT-RUN or BLOCKED, never PASS. No test is changed to make a result look
    better.

### Licensing

> The TPC-H/TPC-DS licensing issue identified in PR #146 remains an
> independent release-compliance concern. My previously stated decision to
> exclude unresolved benchmark implementations from distributed binaries
> remains binding.

Two further requirements apply:

- Keeping DuckDB for compatibility benchmarking does not permit distributing
  an affected production executable.
- The migration must not introduce an equivalent licensing problem.

### Gates

- DF0 completion does not authorize production integration. The maintainer
  reviews the findings, recommendation, proposed dependency changes and plan
  first.
- ADR-002 is not marked operationally superseded until DuckDB has actually
  been removed and the validation gates have passed.
